Agency Collection Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Agency Collection ("the App") is a collection and customer management application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App may process the following information depending on the features you use:

  • Email address and account information used for account sign-in.
  • Information associated with your Google account when you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Customer, outlet, collection, payment, outstanding and related business information that you enter into the App.
  • Photos or images captured or selected for bill and document records when you use the relevant features.
  • Local backup files created by you for backup or restore purposes.
  • Subscription and purchase information provided by Google Play when you use paid subscription features.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Provide and operate the App and its features.
  • Authenticate your account and allow you to sign in.
  • Synchronize your account data between supported devices.
  • Store and restore your business records and backups.
  • Provide bill photo and document-related features.
  • Process and restore subscriptions through Google Play.
  • Provide customer support and respond to support requests.
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized access or misuse.

3. Cloud Services

The App uses Firebase services for account authentication and cloud data synchronization. Depending on the features you use, information associated with your account may be stored in Firebase services.

Cloud data is associated with your authenticated account and is intended to be accessible only to that account through the App's access controls.

4. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, the App uses Google authentication services to authenticate your account. Information provided by Google is used only to provide account authentication and the App's related features.

5. Camera and Photos

The App may request camera or photo access when you use bill-photo or document-related features. Camera or photo access is not required for features that do not use these functions.

6. Backups and Data Sharing

The App may allow you to create local backups and share backup files using services you choose, such as email, messaging applications or cloud storage. When you voluntarily share a file with another service, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply.

7. Google Play Subscriptions

The App uses Google Play Billing for subscription purchases. Subscription payments are processed by Google Play. The App does not directly receive your full payment-card details.

Subscription availability, billing, renewal, cancellation and refunds are subject to Google Play's applicable terms and policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

You may delete your account using the account-management features provided inside the App where available. Account deletion is intended to remove the associated cloud account data handled by the App, subject to technical, legal or service-provider retention requirements.

Local data and backup files stored on your device may require you to manually remove them from the device or storage location.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

11. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services including Firebase, Google Sign-In and Google Play Billing. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
